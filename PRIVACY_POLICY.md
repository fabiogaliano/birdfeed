# Privacy Policy

Birdfeed does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.

## What Birdfeed stores

Birdfeed uses the browser's built-in extension storage (`chrome.storage`) to save your configuration — the set of toggles you have enabled (for example, whether to default to the "Following" tab or hide "For you"). This data is a small JSON object of boolean and enum preference values. It stays on your device, or in your own browser sync account if you have browser sync enabled. It is never sent to Birdfeed, its developer, or any third party.

## What Birdfeed does on the page

Birdfeed runs only on twitter.com, mobile.twitter.com, x.com, and mobile.x.com. On those pages it reads and modifies the DOM to hide or alter UI elements according to your preferences. It does not read or transmit your tweets, DMs, account information, or any other page content off your device.

To support a small number of features (such as per-account behavior), Birdfeed makes a request to Twitter/X's own first-party GraphQL endpoint from within your authenticated session — the same endpoint the page itself uses. The response is used only in your browser and is never stored or transmitted anywhere else.

## What Birdfeed does not do

- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking
- No third-party servers, SDKs, or advertising networks
- No collection of personally identifiable information, location, browsing history, or communications
- No sale or transfer of user data for any purpose
